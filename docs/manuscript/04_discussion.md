# Discussion {#sec:discussion}

A public projection can pair readable figures with machine-readable numerical records. For accepted corpora, this design supports inspection of the exact counts and their source segmentation. Reproducibility guidance motivates preserving the inputs and commands used to derive those records [@sandve2013reproducible]. The figure in @sec:methods depicts the publication method. Its boxes and connectors are conceptual.

Source availability, output custody, and publication completeness are separate checks. A cataloged source can remain uncollected. A byte-identical replay preserves the age and scope of the retained observation.
