# Limitations, Ethics, and Threats to Validity {#sec:limitations}

The fixture contains fictional document metadata and no personal records. Its result depends on how the input represents article and section boundaries. Municipal law, legal importance, policy burden, and resident experience require separate analysis. Extracted text records remain bounded by the exact edition and extraction method.

Dataset documentation should state composition and intended use [@gebru2021datasheets]. Applying that principle here requires identifying the regional producer, evidence date, and source limitations for each reported observation. Legal interpretation, human usefulness, and generated-answer factuality require separate evaluation.
