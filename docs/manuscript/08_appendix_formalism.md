# Appendix: Formal Contracts and Worked Records {#sec:appendix_formalism}

Let each article contribute a count equal to the length of its section-record list. The corpus total sums those counts. The median is the middle sorted count for an odd number of articles and the mean of the two middle counts for an even number. The empty-article count includes every zero.

The fixture CSV provides one row per article. It preserves input order and quotes identifier, number, and title fields. The methods also record explicitly when a PDF profile uses physical pages and extracted lines rather than articles and sections.
